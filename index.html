Clinical context

Smarter support for early laryngeal screening

Laryngeal cancer outcomes improve when warning signs are noticed earlier. Our Final Year Project explores AI-assisted screening that analyzes voice characteristics and visual tissue patterns, then returns an interpretable risk score to guide further clinical attention.

Both modalities use machine learning and deep learning models. Results are for research, education, and triage support — always follow clinical workflows and regulations.

  • Voice feature analysis with live-style waveform recording
  • EfficientNet-based laryngeal image classification
  • Unified Low / Medium / High risk bands with score dashboards
  • Secure accounts, guest mode, and screening history

Voice modality

Record or upload audio, capture lifestyle factors, and receive a screening score out of 100.

Image modality

Upload a laryngeal image for tissue-class prediction, confidence, and plain-language guidance.

Dashboard history

Track past voice and image screenings by day and month with calendar-based filters.

Product features

Everything your screening workflow needs

Built as a polished Expo mobile app with the same maroon and soft-rose theme you see here — designed for clarity, trust, and clinical focus.

01 · Voice Analysis

Record, upload, and score voice samples

Capture audio with a live-style waveform, or upload an existing sample. Add optional clinical context such as gender, smoking, alcohol, and occupation, then run AI voice analysis for a screening score.

  • Microphone recording with waveform feedback
  • Audio file upload support
  • Risk score dashboard (0–100)
  • Clear Low / Medium / High risk bands
02 · Image Analysis

Classify laryngeal images with deep learning

Upload a close-up laryngeal image and analyze it with an EfficientNet tissue classifier. The app returns class labels, confidence, risk band, and simple next-step language for users.

  • Photo library or camera upload
  • Classes such as healthy tissue, vessels, loops, and white patches
  • Confidence-aware “not tissue” safeguards
  • Plain-language explanations for screening results
03 · Dashboard

Review screening history at a glance

The dashboard stores recent voice and image analyses so users can revisit scores over time. Filter by calendar day or month and keep a clear record of past screening activity.

  • Calendar-based history filters
  • Voice and image result cards
  • Low / Medium / High risk indicators
  • Account-linked screening timeline
